## Response Decision Matrix

| Confidence of Finding |	Action Required |
| --------- | ------------- |
| High | Check over the finding manually to determine its exact nature, severity, and potential containment and/or remediation. |
| Medium | Investigate whether the finding is accurate. If so, deal with it as necessary. If not, inspect the system and adjust any flaws that caused it to misjudge the situation. |
| Low | Use automated detections as a baseline to inform manual investigation. After investigation, look over the rules that generated the findings, determine where the errors lie, and adjust as necessary. |
