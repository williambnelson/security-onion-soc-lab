## Entra Evidence Matrix


| Evidence Type | Questions it can Answer |
| --------- | ------------- |
| Identity | Where did the detection come from? What was their IP address? Were they attempting to hide their IP address? Did they access something they weren’t authorized to? When did they sign in? Did they have the proper credentials? |
| Email | What was the content of the email? Where did it come from? Where was it sent to? Was the email from a known risky address? Was the sending pattern suspicious? |
| Endpoint | What endpoints were involved in the detection? What user was involved in the activity? Where were they located during the activity? What services were accessed? Was any sensitive data leaked? |
| Cloud | Were cloud services accessed during the detection? What exactly was accessed? How did they sign in? Who accessed them? Was the cloud environment compromised in any way? Did data exfiltration take place? |
